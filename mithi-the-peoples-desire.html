One Publication, Many Voices

Each member of MITHI brings a different perspective, but we share one goal: to create space for honest stories, meaningful questions, and compassionate conversations about the issues that shape our lives.

MITHI

Journal of Inquiry & Perspective

Ang MITHI ay isang malayang publikasyong nakatuon sa pagpapalalim ng kaisipan, pagsusuri ng mental na kalusugan, kultura, at sining sa parehong wikang Filipino at Ingles.

As an independent bilingual journal, MITHI bridges local perspectives and global dialogues. We publish longform investigations, news updates, critical columns, features, and editorial commentary across our dedicated Filipino Section (Balita & Kolum) and English Section (Feature & Editorial).

Aming Layunin / Our Mission

"Isulong ang malayang diskurso, itaguyod ang panitikang Filipino, at magbigay ng malalim na pagsusuri sa mga suliraning pandaigdig."
